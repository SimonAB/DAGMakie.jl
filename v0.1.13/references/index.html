References

Literature underpinning DAGMakie.jl's causal-diagram conventions, layout rules, and visual grammar. BibTeX keys match the CDCS book references.bib where present.

Causal diagrams, good controls, and do(·) surgery

  • Pearl, J. (2009). Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press, Cambridge. — key pearl2009causality (structural diagrams, d-separation, and do(·) graph surgery)

  • Cinelli, C., Forney, A., & Pearl, J. (2024). A crash course in good and bad controls. Sociological Methods & Research, 53(3), 1071–1104. doi:10.1177/00491241221099552 — key cinelli2024goodbadcontrols (canonical good-control vs bad-control layouts)

  • Hernán, M. A., & Robins, J. M. (2020). Causal Inference: What If. Chapman & Hall/CRC, Boca Raton, FL. — key hernan2020causal (longitudinal and time-varying confounding diagrams)

Role colouring and adjustment sets

  • Textor, J., van der Zander, B., Gilthorpe, M. S., Liśkiewicz, M., & Ellison, G. T. H. (2016). Robust causal inference using directed acyclic graphs: the R package 'dagitty'. International Journal of Epidemiology, 45(6), 1887–1894. doi:10.1093/ije/dyw341 (ancestor and adjustment role colouring used by color_by)

  • van der Zander, B., Liśkiewicz, M., & Textor, J. (2019). Separators and adjustment sets in causal graphs: complete criteria and an algorithmic framework. Artificial Intelligence, 270, 1–40. doi:10.1016/j.artint.2018.12.006 — key vanderzander2019separators (arXiv:1803.00116) (upstream algorithm in CausalInference.jl / CausalDynamics.jl)

SWIGs and interaction diagrams

  • Richardson, T. S., & Robins, J. M. (2013). Single world intervention graphs (SWIGs): A unification of the counterfactual and graphical approaches to causality. arXiv:1201.5658 — key richardson2013single (split-node intervention grammar for dagplot_did_swig and SwigFixed)
